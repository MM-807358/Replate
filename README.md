
# Replate

[Replate](https://mm-807358.github.io/Replate/) is our submission to the CB Hacks hackathon. Our website is about giving businesses an easy way to report a surplus of food, to allow food banks to help provide for people in need more easily.

# The Problem

Businesses often have extra food that they are forced to throw away at the end of the day. This food ends up being completely wasted when it could be given to food banks to help feed the hungry. This leads to increased hunger in the impoverished and potentially increasing food insecurity in those who need it the most.

# The Solution

With Replate, we allow businesses to easily connect with food banks. As a business, you can go onto our site and select your business, select you have a surplus of food and submit. After submitting, anyone looking on the website can see your surplus, helping prevent food waste and taking care of our community.
